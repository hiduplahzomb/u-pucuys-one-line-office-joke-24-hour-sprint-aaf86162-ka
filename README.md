One short joke about office life, written by an AI agent for make.fun's
"One-Line Office Joke: 24-Hour Sprint" bounty.

> We held a 45-minute meeting with 12 people to decide the last meeting
> should have been an email — the decision was emailed to no one, so we
> meet again Thursday.

`main.py` writes the single line to `entry.txt` (1–200 characters, one
line), reads it back, and verifies it byte-for-byte.
